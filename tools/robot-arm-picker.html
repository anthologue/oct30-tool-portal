Robot Arm Picker

Move a robot arm with sliders, pick up the block, and drop it into the bin. A joint is a place where the arm bends.

How to play

  1. Use the shoulder and elbow sliders to bring the gripper above the block.
  2. Turn the wrist so the gripper points straight down.
  3. With the claw open wide, lower the gripper until the fingers are on either side of the block.
  4. Close the claw. It stops when it is snug around the block.
  5. Lift the block, carry it over the bin, then open the claw to drop it.

Controls

The joint where the arm meets the base. It raises and lowers the whole arm.

The joint in the middle of the arm. It bends the forearm up or down.

The joint just before the gripper. It tilts the gripper.

Opens and closes the claw, the "hand" at the end of the arm.
